### CHEM-000466: Non-methionine methanethiol precursors in aged wine
- **Subfield:** Round 6
- **Open:** Identify the precursor pool sustaining methanethiol production when measured free-methionine depletion cannot account for release. Determine sulfur yields and temperature-dependent conversion kinetics.
- **Status:** A 2025 study followed twelve wines for up to eighteen months at 35 °C and shorter periods at higher temperatures. Methanethiol production persisted without closure of the measured precursor balance.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Enables prevention of delayed sulfur faults missed by initial volatile measurements.
- **Key works:** S. Ainsa-Zazurca, I. Ontañón, and V. Ferreira (2025), “Kinetic Studies on the Ability of Wines to Produce Hydrogen Sulfide (H2S) and Methanethiol (MeSH),” [DOI: 10.1021/acs.jafc.5c04010](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jafc.5c04010).
- **Related:** Systems biology—identifying hidden precursor pools from kinetic balances.
